Random Group Generator

Five design directions

All professional / academic, all with the shuffle-then-settle reveal, both sizing modes (number of groups / students per group), save & load, print, the visible “avoid repeat groupings” toggle, and the hidden teacher-only keep-apart rules. Click any one to try it live.

01

Split Studio

Two-pane desktop tool. Dark slate control panel on the left, a large light “stage” for results on the right. Serif headings, single indigo accent. Structured and refined.
Open mockup →
02

Command Deck

Dashboard app-shell. Persistent top toolbar of controls, a saved-sessions rail on the left, results grid in the canvas. Crisp “software product” feel a teacher keeps open all class.
Open mockup →
03

Projector Stage

Presentation-first dark mode for projecting to the class. Controls hide into a drawer during a cinematic reveal; huge glowing group cards readable from the back of the room.
Open mockup →
04

Soft Cards

Calm, warm, Notion-like single column. Rounded cards, paper background, sage/terracotta accents, gentle motion. The most inviting and uncluttered of the five.
Open mockup →
05

Utility Compact

Dense power-user tool. Everything on one no-scroll screen, hairline borders, monospace numbers, keyboard shortcuts. Built for speed and control.
